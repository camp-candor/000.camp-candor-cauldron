## 1. Architectural Purpose Across the Fleet

Previously, `ChronoAgendaRegistry` supported only two temporal cadences on a `ScheduledBeat`: one-shot (`recurringDaily: false` on `isoDate`) or every-day repetition (`recurringDaily: true`). Real worldbuilding and serialized drama require non-daily rhythms:
- Court tribunals convening every Friday (`WEEKLY_MASK: ['FRI']`)
- Caravan supply drops arriving every 3 days (`INTERVAL_DAYS: 3`)
- Nocturnal cult vigils or smuggling runs occurring only on a Full Moon or New Moon (`LUNAR_PHASE: 'FULL_MOON'`)
- Seasonal coronation or harvest festivals anchored to equinoxes and solstices (`SOLAR_SOLSTICE_EQUINOX`)
- Holiday or siege blackout exceptions (`blackoutDates`, `blackoutWeekdays`, `series_blackout_dates`) that either cancel a single occurrence cleanly (`SKIP`) or postpone it to the next open business day (`DEFER_NEXT_VALID_DAY`)

## 2. Core Recurrence Rule Contract

Each `ScheduledBeat` in `chronology.scheduled_beats` accepts an optional `recurrenceRule` object (while legacy `recurringDaily: true/false` normalizes automatically via `normalizeBeatRecurrenceRule` / `normalizeEngineRecurrenceRule`):

| Field | Type / Constraint | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `frequency` | `'ONCE' \| 'DAILY' \| 'WEEKLY_MASK' \| 'INTERVAL_DAYS' \| 'LUNAR_PHASE' \| 'SOLAR_SOLSTICE_EQUINOX'` | Selects the calendar evaluation strategy. |
| `weekdayMask` | `Array<'MON'..'SUN'>` (`1..7`) | Required when `frequency === 'WEEKLY_MASK'`. Matches specific Gregorian days of the week. |
| `intervalDays` | Integer `1..365` | Required when `frequency === 'INTERVAL_DAYS'`. Fires every `N` days from `beat.isoDate` (`daysDelta % intervalDays === 0`). |
| `lunarPhase` | `'NEW_MOON' \| 'FIRST_QUARTER' \| 'FULL_MOON' \| 'LAST_QUARTER'` | Required when `frequency === 'LUNAR_PHASE'`. Matches synodic lunar phase windows. |
| `solarMarkers` | `Array<'VERNAL_EQUINOX' \| 'SUMMER_SOLSTICE' \| 'AUTUMN_EQUINOX' \| 'WINTER_SOLSTICE'>` | Required when `frequency === 'SOLAR_SOLSTICE_EQUINOX'`. Matches `-03-20`, `-06-21`, `-09-22`, `-12-21`. |
| `blackoutDates` | `Array<YYYY-MM-DD>` (`0..128`) | Beat-specific holiday/siege exception dates validated against Gregorian leap-year rules (`[INVALID_BLACKOUT_DATE]`). |
| `blackoutWeekdays` | `Array<'MON'..'SUN'>` (`0..6`) | Suppresses or defers occurrences landing on excluded weekdays (e.g., sabbath rest days on `'SUN'` for an `INTERVAL_DAYS` beat). |
| `blackoutShiftPolicy` | `'SKIP' \| 'DEFER_NEXT_VALID_DAY'` | `'SKIP'` cancels the occurrence (and suppresses its `reserveCooldownTicks`); `'DEFER_NEXT_VALID_DAY'` shifts it forward `1..7` days to the first non-blacked-out date. |
| `untilIsoDate` | Optional `YYYY-MM-DD` (`>= beat.isoDate`) | Inclusive upper calendar bound (`[RECURRENCE_UNTIL_BEFORE_START]` if earlier than `isoDate`). |
| `maxOccurrences` | Optional integer `1..1000` | Retires the recurring beat after firing `N` times during a simulation run. |
| `series_blackout_dates` | Optional `Array<YYYY-MM-DD>` on `chronology` | Series-wide blackout calendar applied across all recurring beats in the canon. |

## 3. Date-Time Engineering Highlights

1. **Tomohiko Sakamoto Pure Integer Weekday Resolver:** `resolveGregorianWeekday` / `resolveSpatialWeekday` / `resolveEngineGregorianWeekday` compute exact Gregorian weekdays (`MON..SUN`) across leap years (`2024-02-29 -> THU`) and century transitions without touching host `Date.now()` or locale settings.
2. **Synodic Lunar Phase Cycle With Nocturnal Optical Coupling:** All three repositories share identical integer synodic constants (`SYNODIC_MONTH_SECONDS = 2551443`, reference New Moon `2000-01-06T18:14:00Z = 947182440` UTC seconds), computing integer `illuminationBps` (`0..10000`). In `301.spatial`, `NIGHT` and `DUSK` occurrences scale `nightLuminanceBoostBps` (up to `+1500` bps) and `nightOpticalRangeBoostBps` (up to `+2000` bps) so Full Moon courtyard scenes admit wider witness sightlines than New Moon scenes.
3. **Zero Phantom Lockouts on Skipped Blackouts:** When a recurring occurrence hits a blackout date under `blackoutShiftPolicy: 'SKIP'`, `evaluateChronoAgendaWindow` suppresses both the due trigger AND the pre-deadline `reserveCooldownTicks` window on that day, preventing orphaned filler lockouts.
4. **Lookback Deferral Resolution (`DEFER_NEXT_VALID_DAY`):** When a Friday tribunal is blacked out on `2026-10-30` with `DEFER_NEXT_VALID_DAY`, the engine shifts the occurrence to `2026-10-31` (`SAT`), activates `reserveCooldownTicks` on Saturday prior to `startTimeHm`, stamps `Deferred-From-Blackout: 2026-10-30` into the compiled `.fountain` screenplay, and records the deferral in `index.json`.
5. **Gate 0 Dead-End Schedule Trap (`[RECURRENCE_ZERO_MATCH_IN_HORIZON]`):** If an author configures `untilIsoDate` and `blackoutDates` such that a recurring beat can never fire even once, `43.compile.unit` fails closed during Gate 0 compilation.

## 6. High-Value Missing Elements Analysis

While `CalendarRecurrenceGrammar` solves weekday masks, fixed day intervals, synodic lunar phases, solar equinoxes/solstices, and holiday blackouts (`SKIP` and `DEFER_NEXT_VALID_DAY`), six deeper temporal capabilities remain unaddressed and offer major value for long-horizon narrative worlds:

1. **Ordinal Monthly Recurrence Rules (`Nth` Weekday or Day-of-Month):**
   - *Current Gap:* `WEEKLY_MASK` fires every week on matching weekdays, and `INTERVAL_DAYS` steps by fixed day counts, but neither can express "the First Monday of every month" or "Payday on the last Friday of every month."
   - *Why It Matters:* Civic councils, rent/tax collection, and guild elections in serialized fiction follow monthly ordinal calendars rather than every-week or fixed-day intervals.
2. **Cascading Conflict Resolution When a Deferred Beat Collides With Another Tentpole:**
   - *Current Gap:* `DEFER_NEXT_VALID_DAY` shifts a blacked-out Friday appointment onto Saturday at the same `startTimeHm`, even if Saturday already hosts an authored high-priority appointment in that room or with those participants.
   - *Why It Matters:* Deferring a court session onto a Saturday morning that already has a scheduled royal wedding at `09:00` creates a same-slot venue/cast collision unless the deferral resolver checks existing appointments and advances to the next conflict-free slot or time window.
3. **Weather-Coupled Nocturnal Lunar Attenuation in `301.spatial`:**
   - *Current Gap:* `computeSpatialLunarOptics` grants up to `+1500` luminance bps and `+2000` optical range bps on a `FULL_MOON` night assuming clear skies.
   - *Why It Matters:* A Full Moon during a heavy fog bank or thunderstorm should have its lunar optical boost attenuated by atmospheric occlusion so stealth infiltrations react to combined astronomy + weather state.
4. **Recurring Cast Attendance Rotation (`ParticipantPool` Quotas):**
   - *Current Gap:* `participantIds` is a static list repeated identically on every occurrence of a recurring beat.
   - *Why It Matters:* Guard watch rotations, rotating senate chairs, or weekly market stalls become repetitive if the exact same three characters attend every single occurrence instead of rotating deterministically by `occurrenceIndex` from a role pool.
5. **Conductor Multi-Occurrence Series Arc Compliance in `400.conductor`:**
   - *Current Gap:* `400.conductor` (`reconcileEpisodeAgendaCompliance`) reconciles beat presence by `beat_id`, but does not yet parse `Recurrence-Rule:`, `Occurrence-Index:`, or `Deferred-From-Blackout:` headers to audit whether all expected occurrences (`#1..#K`) across a multi-week episode stack fired or were legitimately skipped by a blackout.
   - *Why It Matters:* If a weekly Friday court session fires on Week 1 (`#1`) but is dropped on Week 2 (`#2`), a simple `beat_id` check sees `court_session` present at least once and passes silently unless Conductor audits per-occurrence keys (`beat_id@effectiveIsoDate`).
6. **Custom World Calendar Epochs Beyond Proleptic Gregorian:**
   - *Current Gap:* Dates are strictly Gregorian `YYYY-MM-DD` with a single 29.53-day moon.
   - *Why It Matters:* High-fantasy or sci-fi settings often feature dual moons with beat-frequency conjunctions (`twin-moon eclipse`) or 10-day festival weeks that map onto internal epoch seconds.
