### Pillar I: Alethic Aesthetics (The Physics of the Curse)
* **The Materiality of Scarred Earth:**
  * Matter is rough and desiccated. The ground is the *"dust to which you shall return,"* cracking underfoot with audible fracture sounds.
  * Metal is ancient bronze and iron that heats under the sun until it blisters the palm; it rusts from the cosmic decay of time rather than mere chemical oxidation.
  * Skin is weathered leather, cracked by desert winds, sweating under the physical burden of labor.
* **The Shadow Hardness (The Outer Darkness):**
  * Light is divine revelation: either blinding white or searing red, casting shadows of **Absolute Black** (`#000000`).
  * Shadows are not the absence of photons; they are *Sheol*—a physical drop in ambient temperature and audio presence where protection ceases.
* **The Temporal Weight:**
  * Motion is jagged and convulsive. Divine entities do not interpolate; they snap discontinuously across the frame.
  * Critical impacts execute trauma time dilation, prolonging the visual weight of catastrophic events.

---

### Pillar II: Deontic Aesthetics (The Law of the Tablet)
* **The Accusing Interface:**
  * Clean vector overlays are prohibited. Floating minimaps and damage integers are banned as immersion-breaking abstractions.
  * Health is tracked via visible bodily wounds or the sputtering wick of an in-world oil lamp. Maps are physical, cracking scrolls held in the hands.
  * Menus slam downward onto the screen with the physical weight and audible thud of a stone tablet, scattering dust particles.
* **The Typography of Judgment:**
  * Text is chiseled into granite (`STONE_TABLET`), scrawled across vellum in frantic ink (`SCRIBE_BLOOD`), or rendered as smoldering embers (`BURNING_COAL`).

---

### Pillar III: Axiological Aesthetics (The Valuation of Wrath)
* **The Color Grading (The Wine Press / `LUT_BRASS_SKY`):**
  * The world is bleached by desert heat: desaturated ochres, blinding whites, and iron browns.
  * The sky is the color of beaten brass (Deuteronomy 28:23).
  * **Arterial Blood Red** is the sole saturated color permitted in the simulation, popping against the beige world like a signal fire.
  * A permanent heat-haze distortion shader ripples across the horizon: *"The earth trembles at His presence."*
* **The Sonic Burden:**
  * System alerts sound as raw animalistic ram's horn (*shofar*) blasts cutting through the mix.
  * Ambient loops consist of turning millstones, tectonic stress, and grinding granite.
  * High-trauma events trigger absolute silence paired with a high-pitched tinnitus tone.
* **The Grain of Judgment:**
  * Visual noise is atmospheric dust, swirling ash, and cracked painting varnish. Intense holy light burns out the film margins.

---

### Pillar IV: Epistemic Aesthetics (The Architecture of the Veil)
* **The Glitch as Revelation:**
  * System breakdown is spiritual vision: textures sweat blood, peripheral screen margins catch fire like burning film, and holy presences trigger blinding white-out flashes that leave burned afterimages.
  * Damage reveals the spiritual realm rather than hiding it.
* **The Veiled Horizon:**
  * Visibility is occluded by walls of swirling sand (*Dust Storm*), thick greasy *Smoke of Sacrifice*, or supernatural *Egyptian Darkness* that actively swallows light rays.

## III. The Dictionary of Anathema (The Asset Manifest)

Detailed asset profiles binding theology to visual and acoustic rendering rules:

1. **The Orange (`item_food_orange` / The Sphere of Temptation):**
   * *Description:* A globe of burning color in a gray world, pitted like the moon, smelling of stinging acid. Peeling tears skin and sprays mist; the cloying sweetness leaves an unquenchable thirst.
   * *Render:* High saturation orange, high roughness, internal subsurface scattering.
2. **The Microphone (`prop_tech_mic` / The Snare of Words):**
   * *Description:* A cold wire-mesh cage on a pedestal capturing human breath and converting spirit into dead electrical signals; the watchful ear of the King listening for bedroom treason.
   * *Render:* Dark iron, sharp specular highlights, subtle vertex displacement vibration when receiving audio.
3. **The Computer (`prop_tech_computer` / The Tablet of Babel):**
   * *Description:* A plastic monolith humming with captive demon heat, its unblinking glass eye offering infinite screaming knowledge in exchange for the soul; the corpse-light of a new Babel.
   * *Render:* Cool blue screen emission (4000K), matte dusty casing, audible mechanical fan loop.
4. **The Clock (`prop_decor_clock` / The Millstone):**
   * *Description:* A circular prison of time with scythe-like hands slicing moments into dead segments; a grinding wheel wearing down mountains and counting steps to the grave.
   * *Render:* Yellowed parchment dial, worm-eaten oak case, heavy irregular ticking with reverberant decay.
5. **The iPhone (`item_tech_phone` / The Mirror of Narcissus):**
   * *Description:* Polished black stone reflecting the fool who holds it; an idol carried in the pocket demanding bowed-neck worship, summoning envy with a finger touch.
   * *Render:* Perfectly smooth zero-roughness glass, high reflectivity, normal smudge maps resembling greasy fingerprints.
6. **The Jiggy Jar (`prop_container_jar` / The Vessel of Abomination):**
   * *Description:* Clouded glass vessel holding the discarded shame of the secret place; suspended amber and congealed blood standing as a monument to stagnation.
   * *Render:* Thick distorted glass (IOR 1.5), murky volumetric interior fog.
7. **The Mousepad (`prop_desk_mat` / The Plain of Idle Hands):**
   * *Description:* A rectangle of rubberized flesh absorbing palm sweat; the digital threshing floor where hands trace endless circles, reaping only pixels.
   * *Render:* Matte black fabric, visible normal-map weave, frayed edges.
8. **The Radio (`prop_tech_radio` / The Box of Winds):**
   * *Description:* A wooden crate trapping spirits of the air, crackling with cosmic void static, bringing rumors of war into the sanctuary of the home.
   * *Render:* Raw wood grain, vibrating fabric speaker cone, warm orange filament tuning bulb.
9. **Nail Clippers (`item_tool_clippers` / The Severing Jaw):**
   * *Description:* Cold spring-loaded steel cutting away dead horn with a violent snap; a reminder that wild flesh must be pruned lest it become a claw.
   * *Render:* Brushed stainless steel, sharp specular glints, sound profile boosted +10dB with a bone-crack layer.
10. **Ink Pen (`item_tool_pen` / The Quill of Judgment):**
    * *Description:* A cylinder carrying black blood that bleeds onto parchment, carving binding debts and death warrants that outlive the hand that held it.
    * *Render:* Glossy black plastic, wet specular normal map on fresh text drying over 5 seconds.
11. **The City (`zone_urban_center` / Babel):**
    * *Description:* A stone mountain built to challenge heaven; a smoke-choked labyrinth of unwashed crowds and sewers where strangers huddle against the dark.
    * *Render:* Looming forced-perspective architecture, gray/brick-red palette, flocking crowd cloth simulations.
12. **The Town (`zone_rural_settlement` / Bethlehem):**
    * *Description:* Mud-brick huts clinging like barnacles to beaten earth, fragile and open to heat and starlight; a place where all sins are known.
    * *Render:* Low-poly mud-brick structures, high-detail ground texture (straw, dust, chipped stone).
13. **The Swamp (`zone_wetland` / Sheol):**
    * *Description:* The chaotic mixture where earth and water refuse to separate; moss-draped cypress knees standing over black water that hides the teeth of Leviathan.
    * *Render:* Ground-level volumetric fog, high water turbidity, green-brown canopy lighting filters.

## V. Technical Implementation Pipelines

1. **The Shader of Dust (`The_Curse`):** A global post-process shader overlaying a procedural dirt map across all scene textures, darkening crevice ambient occlusion and bleaching surface color based on direct sunlight intensity.
2. **The Audio of Weight (`The_Burden`):** A master DSP bus boosting sub-frequencies on physical impacts, adding grit distortion to footsteps, and applying an ear-ringing tinnitus shelf after explosive sound cues.
3. **The Jeremiah Parameter (LLM Fine-Tuning):** Conditioning drafting prompts to purge comforting vocabulary, mandating terms of age, weight, dust, and divine consequence.
