# MurkWaste Canonical Dossier: Old One-Eye, Torvin & The MurkWaste Barony

A canonical world-building and entity record establishing the ecology, characters, technology, and deontic covenants of the MurkWaste Barony, transmediated from the script `"The Appointment"` (`SC-MurkWaste-01`).

---

## 1. Geographic & Environmental Overview: The MurkWaste Barony

* **Territory & Setting:** The MurkWaste Barony is an expansive wetland ecosystem defined by stagnant, obsidian-black waters, ancient ironroot mangrove forests, and perpetual low-altitude mist that diffuses direct sunlight into a pale, gray-green luminescence.
* **Sensory Signature:** High ambient moisture approaching saturation; rancid reptilian musk, petrichor, and sulfur rising from undisturbed anaerobic sediment; acoustic dead zones where heavy foliage and mist swallow high-frequency sound, leaving only low water rumbles and sudden bubbling punctures.
* **Topological Landmarks:**
  * *The Skiff Corridors:* Winding waterways navigable exclusively by shallow-draft wooden watercraft poled by experienced marsh guides.
  * *The Tyrant Shrines:* Giant, half-submerged calcified skulls of ancient mega-fauna that serve as territorial markers, resting points, and physical reminders of ecological mortality.
  * *The Stasis Lagoons:* Deep, wind-sheltered pools where ancient alpha predators maintain established sunning coordinates and hold territorial sovereignty.

## 3. Character Profiles

### Old Man Torvin (The Wounded Veteran)
* **Age & Appearance:** Mid-70s; heavily weathered skin etched with a network of swamp scars; eyes narrowed, vigilant, and patient.
* **The Prosthetic:** Left leg amputated below the knee, replaced with a field-crafted prosthetic carved from dense **Ironroot Mangrove** wood and reinforced with cured alligator hide.
* **Character History & Hubris:** In his youth, Torvin operated under the arrogance of human dominion, believing his gator-spear represented the ultimate authority in the swamp. Confronting Old One-Eye twenty years prior cost him his limb, his pride, and his nerve for an entire winter. The encounter broke his hubris, teaching him the foundational virtue of the wetland: **Respect**.
* **Role:** Master hunter, skiff poler, and philosophical mentor. He approaches the marsh not with violence, but as a negotiator keeping a delayed appointment.

### Finn (The Apprentice)
* **Age & Appearance:** 16 years old; nervous, white-knuckled, clutching a newly forged gator-spear.
* **Psychological Arc:** Begins the journey viewing swamp legends through the romanticized lens of conquest and monster slaying. Facing the quiet majesty and sheer physical bulk of Old One-Eye re-anchors his perception: true mastery is not conquering nature, but achieving the wisdom to live alongside its perils.

## 4. Material Culture & Hunting Technology

* **The Swamp Skiff:** Flat-bottomed, narrow, weathered wooden skiff engineered to glide silently across zero-current obsidian water. Propelled by an iron-capped mangrove pole, avoiding noisy mechanical engines or splashing paddles.
* **The Gator-Spear:** A heavy, leaf-bladed thrusting spear forged with barbs to hold thick reptilian hide. In the hands of a novice, an instrument of fear; in the hands of a master, a tool kept at rest unless survival is threatened.
* **Mud-Skipper Hog Heart:** A dense, high-protein biological offering harvested from marsh swine. Used traditionally as a blood tribute to acknowledge leviathan territory and secure unhindered transit through ancient breeding sloughs.

## 5. Deontic Axioms & The Law of Respect

In the MurkWaste Barony, human survival is governed by strict cultural and moral axioms:
1. **The Prohibition of Hubris:** The swamp is not a passive warehouse of meat and hides. Any hunter who treats an alpha beast as mere resource rather than sovereign authority is culled by the ecosystem.
2. **The Principle of the Appointment:** Legendary beasts are not hunted; they are met at their established geographic thresholds.
3. **The Fair Trade Covenant:** *"The swamp provides, and sometimes the swamp takes its due."* A hunter must honor the balance—offering sacrifice and showing restraint rather than seeking mindless conquest.
