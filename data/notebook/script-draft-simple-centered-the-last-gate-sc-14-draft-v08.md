# THE LAST GATE
*Scene SC-14 — Draft v08 (Simple Centered Layout)*

---

### Logline
An aging traveler, once known by a grand name now worn thin by years on the road, arrives at the last checkpoint before the plaza's edge — and, through his own insistence on being recognized, talks himself directly into the one admission he came here to avoid. He returns once more, ready to take by force what he couldn't be given.

### Theme
Being technically who you once were is not the same as being someone now — and matched strength, in the end, proves nothing either of them didn't already know.

## Setting

**THE LAST GATE, VELADERO CROSSING**

*A checkpoint of indeterminate origin, standing where a stone causeway narrows to meet the edge of a sun-bleached plaza. Once part customs house, part confessional — the two functions long since blurred by disuse. Weathered limestone walls, streaked black where old rain has run down through peeling gilt scrollwork that once framed a coat of arms now worn past recognition. A single low wooden counter, its surface grooved smooth by decades of hands and ledgers. Behind it, shelves of identical brass tokens, dulled with handling.*

*Heat sits heavy and unmoving in the archway. Dust drifts in the low afternoon light rather than falling. Somewhere beyond the gate, the plaza hums faintly with a life the checkpoint no longer shares — voices, a distant instrument, the ordinary sound of people who arrived successfully. The Gatekeeper sits unbothered by any of it, as though the hum outside were weather, not company.*

*The Traveler arrives from the causeway side, carrying almost nothing.*

---

## Beat One — Arrival

*The Traveler, once impeccably dressed, now in clothes that were fine a decade and several roads ago, approaches with the practiced posture of someone who has arrived at many counters before.*

<p align="center"><b>TRAVELER</b></p>
<p align="center">Good evening. I believe I'm expected.</p>

*The Gatekeeper, without looking up, turns a page in a ledger that seems to have no end.*

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center">Everyone is expected. That's not the same as being permitted.</p>

---

## Beat Two — The Empty Folio

<p align="center"><b>TRAVELER</b></p>
<p align="center"><i>(a small, practiced laugh)</i></p>
<p align="center">Naturally. I have my papers.</p>

*He produces an ornate, sun-bleached folio. The Gatekeeper accepts it without interest, opens it, finds it empty, sets it down as one might set down something already known to be broken.*

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center">These are very handsome papers.</p>

<p align="center"><b>TRAVELER</b></p>
<p align="center">They were drawn up by the finest office in the capital.</p>

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center">I don't doubt it. They're also blank.</p>

*(a beat)*

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center">What else have you brought?</p>

---

## Beat Three — The Trap He Sets Himself

*The Traveler hesitates for the first time — searches his coat, his memory, the horizon behind him, as though the answer might be found in any of those places.*

<p align="center"><b>TRAVELER</b></p>
<p align="center">I brought... my name. Surely that still carries weight somewhere on this road.</p>

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center">It carries exactly as much weight as anyone in the plaza still chooses to lend it. Ask them yourself, if you like. I only stamp what's real.</p>

---

## Beat Four — The Confession

<p align="center"><b>TRAVELER</b></p>
<p align="center"><i>(quieter now, the performance slipping)</i></p>
<p align="center">I was received once. In rooms far grander than this one. I was toasted. I was — <i>(he stops himself)</i> — I was somebody people wrote about.</p>

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center"><i>(not unkindly)</i></p>
<p align="center">I believe you. I'm not asking who you were. I'm asking what you're carrying now, standing at my counter, tonight.</p>

*A long silence. The Traveler sets down his folio, empty as it is, as though laying down something heavier.*

---

## Beat Five — The Name, Returned

<p align="center"><b>TRAVELER</b></p>
<p align="center"><i>(barely audible)</i></p>
<p align="center">Nothing. I'm carrying nothing.</p>

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center"><i>(gently, closing the ledger)</i></p>
<p align="center">Good. Now we can begin honestly.</p>

*(a beat, almost kind)*

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center">You said your name still carries weight. I'll tell you plainly what it's worth here: exactly as much as this — <i>(taps the empty folio)</i> — until you decide to be someone standing in front of me instead of someone written about somewhere else.</p>

*The Gatekeeper reaches beneath the counter and produces a single plain token, unremarkable, unadorned, and sets it in front of the Traveler.*

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center">This won't get you through the gate. But it'll get you a seat, a drink, and an evening where no one asks you to be anyone at all.</p>

*The Traveler looks at it a long moment. Then picks it up — not gratefully, not bitterly, simply because there is nothing else left to do with his hands.*

<p align="center"><b>TRAVELER</b></p>
<p align="center"><i>(a faint, real smile)</i></p>
<p align="center">I suppose that will have to be enough.</p>

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center">It usually is, in the end.</p>

*The Traveler turns and walks toward the plaza's edge, no longer carrying the folio at all. He leaves it on the counter. The Gatekeeper does not call after him.*

---

## Beat Six — The Return

*The Traveler is back at the counter — no folio this time, no token. His coat is gone; what remains is plain, practical, unadorned. He has come to take the gate by force.*

<p align="center"><b>TRAVELER</b></p>
<p align="center">You said it yourself. You only stamp what's real.</p>

*(a beat)*

<p align="center">This is real.</p>

*Steel clears leather. The Gatekeeper doesn't reach for the ledger this time — he reaches beneath the counter for something that isn't a token.*

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center">Then let's see what that's worth.</p>

---

## Fight Construction — Sub-Storyboard System

*Choreography below is built in locked beat-sequences rather than single panels. Dialogue occurs only in resolution beats and the gaps between sequences — never inside an active phase. Both combatants are matched in skill throughout.*

---

### ⚔ SEQUENCE 1 — Opening Test *(2-beat: Anticipation → Resolution)*

```
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
 A · ANTICIPATION
   Traveler shifts weight back, blade drawn low and
   angled — testing distance, not committing.

 C · RESOLUTION
   Gatekeeper's counter-parry meets steel at the
   midpoint — blades locked at a dead stop, then both
   step back to identical distance. Hold 2 frames on
   the lock. Reset, not retreat.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

<p align="center"><b>TRAVELER</b></p>
<p align="center"><i>(circling, testing)</i></p>
<p align="center">You're better than a man behind a counter has any right to be.</p>

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center">I didn't build this gate to hand it to whoever asked politely.</p>

---

### ⚔ SEQUENCE 2 — First Real Commitment *(3-beat: A / B / C)*

```
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
 A · ANTICIPATION
   Gatekeeper pivots off the counter itself —
   full-body wind-up, weight fully loaded onto the
   front foot.

 B · IMPACT
   Blade grazes the Traveler's shoulder — first
   blood, minor. Hold 2 frames.

 C · RESOLUTION
   Traveler absorbs it without breaking stance,
   already resetting.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

> **Board Note `[REQUIRED]`** — Every impact beat across all sequences must independently pass the blackout test.

<p align="center"><b>TRAVELER</b></p>
<p align="center"><i>(a hiss of breath, almost amused)</i></p>
<p align="center">There it is.</p>

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center">Don't sound so pleased. It's not a compliment.</p>

---

### ⚔ SEQUENCE 3 — Traded Blows *(4-beat: A / B1 / B2 / C)*

```
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
 A · ANTICIPATION
   Both commit simultaneously — mirrored stance,
   weight low, blades drawn back on opposite sides.

 B1 · IMPACT (LEFT)
   Split-screen, left panel: the Traveler's strike
   connecting. Hold 2 frames.

 B2 · IMPACT (RIGHT)
   Split-screen, right panel: the Gatekeeper's
   counter-strike connecting at the same instant.
   Hold 2 frames.

 C · RESOLUTION
   Both stagger the same distance, same angle —
   confirmation of matched skill.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

*(No dialogue in this sequence's resolution — both are too winded.)*

---

### ⚔ SEQUENCE 4 — The Exhaustion Beat *(3-beat: A / B / C, degraded)*

```
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
 A · ANTICIPATION (LATE)
   Neither anticipates cleanly now — both wind-ups
   visibly late, sloppier than before.

 B · IMPACT (MUTED)
   A clash that ends in a shared stumble rather than
   a clean hit — no impact "wins" this exchange.

 C · RESOLUTION (EXTENDED)
   Both break apart, breathing hard, weapons lowered
   but not sheathed. Hold 3 seconds.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center"><i>(genuinely winded, almost laughing)</i></p>
<p align="center">How long has it been since someone made you work this hard?</p>

<p align="center"><b>TRAVELER</b></p>
<p align="center"><i>(same)</i></p>
<p align="center">Long enough I forgot I could still do it.</p>

*(a beat, weapons still raised)*

<p align="center"><b>TRAVELER</b> (cont'd)</p>
<p align="center">Why haven't you finished it?</p>

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center">Same reason you haven't. We're not actually here to kill each other.</p>

<p align="center"><b>TRAVELER</b></p>
<p align="center">Then why are we doing this at all?</p>

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center">Because you needed to hear it from your own arm, not my mouth.</p>

---

### ⚔ SEQUENCE 5 — The Decision, Not the Kill *(5-beat: A / B1 / B2 / B3 / C)*

```
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
 A · ANTICIPATION
   Traveler raises his blade for a final committed
   strike.

 B1 · CONTACT BEGINS
   Blade begins its arc — no longer stoppable.

 B2 · THE CHOICE
   The Gatekeeper's stance shifts from defense to
   acceptance.

 B3 · IMPACT (NON-LETHAL)
   Blade lands at the flat against his shoulder.
   Hold 4 frames.

 C · RESOLUTION
   The Traveler doesn't press the advantage. Blade
   lowers on its own.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

<p align="center"><b>GATEKEEPER</b></p>
<p align="center"><i>(breathing hard, lowering his own weapon)</i></p>
<p align="center">There it is. That's what you actually brought.</p>

<p align="center"><b>TRAVELER</b></p>
<p align="center"><i>(also breathing hard, a real, exhausted laugh)</i></p>
<p align="center">Took me long enough.</p>

*They stand there a moment — not friends, not enemies, just two people who found out, the hard way, they were never that different.*

*Lights fade.*

## Production Notes

* **Staging:** Simultaneously bureaucratic and dreamlike; no clear indication of nation, era, or authority.
* **Costume:** Once-fine, meticulously maintained despite wear in Beats 1–5; Beat Six's plain dress marks a deliberate break from that language.
* **Sound:** Minimal underscore through Beat Five; Beat Six introduces steel, breath, and impact as its first real sound design.
* **Lighting:** Warm but failing light through Beats 1–5; Beat Six's fight may need its own harder lighting pass before returning to the original fade — currently unspecified.
* **Beat-count logic:** 2-beat (Sequence 1), 3-beat (Sequences 2 and 4), 4-beat (Sequence 3), 5-beat (Sequence 5) — chosen by what each moment needs, not applied uniformly.
* **Dialogue placement discipline:** Speech occurs only in resolution beats and gaps between sequences, never inside an active phase.
* **Silhouette compliance:** Every impact beat must independently pass the blackout test before boarding proceeds.
