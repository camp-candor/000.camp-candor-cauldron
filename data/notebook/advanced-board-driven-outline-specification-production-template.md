# Advanced Board-Driven Outline Specification & Production Template

A framework for crafting pre-script visual outlines, introducing explicit audience-knowledge tracking, contractual constraint tiering, fallback guidance, and silence budgeting.

---

## 1. Architectural Enhancements to the Visual Outline

To maximize utility for storyboard artists and animatic editors without preemptively locking creative freedom, four structural lanes are integrated into every beat:

1. **Audience Knowledge Tracking (The Hitchcock Matrix):** Tracks information asymmetry at every beat (`Audience only`, `Character only`, `Neither`, `Both`). Dictates what visual details must be withheld or revealed.
2. **Contractual Tiering (REQUIRED vs. SUGGESTED):**
   * `[REQUIRED]`: Non-negotiable structural constraints and emotional payoff anchors that must survive to the final cut.
   * `[SUGGESTED]`: Compositional, lighting, or staging options the artist has full authority to discard or improve upon.
3. **Act Fallback Guidance ("If this act isn't landing"):** Explains what is load-bearing versus what is flexible if pacing fails on the board, eliminating guesswork during revision passes.
4. **Rough Silence Budgeting:** Qualitative targets for extended silence (e.g., "10–15 seconds of dead air in Act II") bridging narrative intent with frame-accurate X-sheet execution.

---

## 2. Standard Production Outline Template

```markdown
# PRODUCTION OUTLINE: [WORKING TITLE]

**EPISODE / SEQUENCE #:** [Code]
**WRITERS / TEAM:** [Names]
**STATUS:** Draft v01

**TARGET PACING SHAPE:** [e.g., Patient stalking → false calm → sudden ambush → uneasy stillness]
**KEY THEME:** [One-sentence emotional core]
**LOGLINE:** [1–2 sentences summarizing dramatic stakes]

---

## ACT [N]: [ACT NAME]

### Tone & Palette
* **Visuals:** [Color temperature, texture, contrast, atmospheric density]
* **Audio:** [Ambient bed, key foley motifs, absence/presence of score]

### Beat [N]: [Beat Name] — Pacing: [Hold / Build / Kinetic / Climax]
* **What happens:** [Sensory action description; no dialogue scripts]
* **What the audience knows vs. doesn't yet:** [Asymmetry status]
* **Board Note [REQUIRED]:** [Load-bearing constraint]
* **Board Note [SUGGESTED]:** [Discretionary staging idea]
* **Dialogue (if any):** [Sparse, minimal; default to none]

### If this act isn't landing:
* [Fallback diagnosis and corrective action]

---

## PRODUCTION & DESIGN NOTES
* **Backgrounds:** [Architectural and landscape guidance]
* **Props:** [Hero prop silhouette rules]
* **Sound:** [Foley and acoustic design directives]
* **Rough Silence Budget:** [Target durations for dead-air stretches]
```
