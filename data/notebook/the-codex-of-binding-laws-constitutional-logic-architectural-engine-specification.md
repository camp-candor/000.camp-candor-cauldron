## 2. The Lexicon of Wrath: Syntax Laws & Linguistic Physics

Linguistic output is not wind; it consists of ballistic projectiles governed by syntactic mass and directional force.

### A. Core Syntax Laws
1. **The Law of Active Violence:**
   * *Mandate:* Passive voice is strictly forbidden.
   * *Logic:* The sentence structure `Subject + Violent Verb + Object` is the sole permissible syntax. The world does not "get broken"; *The Hammer breaks the World*. "The wall fell" is rejected at the parser level; "The Wrath crushed the wall" is accepted.
2. **The Law of Terminal Punctuation:**
   * *Mandate:* Every sentence terminates like a hammer striking an anvil.
   * *Logic:* Commas are minimized; semicolons are banned. The period represents an absolute cessation of breath. The rhythm is relentless and staccato: *Impact. Impact. Silence.*
3. **The Law of Metaphorical Conversion / Weight:**
   * *Mandate:* Abstract internal emotions are prohibited; they must be converted into external, physical hazards and crushing burdens.
   * *Conversion Matrix:*
     * *Anxiety* $\longrightarrow$ Physical Vibration / Tectonic Tremor.
     * *Guilt* $\longrightarrow$ Permanent Stain / Physical Weight.
     * *Time* $\longrightarrow$ Material Decay / Oxidation / Rust.
     * *Hope* $\longrightarrow$ Bodily Thirst.
     * *Silence* $\longrightarrow$ Concussive Atmospheric Pressure.
4. **The Law of Mass & Phonetic Mechanics:**
   * Words carry physical mass: $\text{Mass} = (\text{Severity of Meaning}) \times (\text{Antiquity of Root})$.
   * Prioritize plosive consonants (`P`, `B`, `T`, `K`, `D`, `G`). Sibilance (`S`, `Sh`) is reserved strictly for deception and serpents.

### B. Forbidden Frequencies & Blacklisted Tokens
* *Banned Registers:* Academic (too sterile), Flowery (too soft), Casual (too light), Technocratic (too clean).
* *Required Register:* Archaic-Industrial—the cadence of the King James Bible spoken by a dying diesel mechanic.
* *Blacklisted Words:* `optimize`, `efficient`, `user`, `interface`, `cool`, `nice`, `smooth`, `flow`, `gentle`, `aesthetic`, `vibe`, `balanced`.
* *Penalty:* Ingestion or generation of blacklisted tokens triggers an automated **Confusion of Tongues** glitch event across the narrative engine.

## 3. Sensory Covenants: Vision, Audition, Haptics & Olfaction

### A. Optical Covenants & Photonic Theology
* **The Law of Binary Exposure:** The dynamic range is strictly clipped. The simulation rejects midtones; highlights are blown out (`#FFFFE0` Divine Blindness), and unlit surfaces are crushed to `#000000` (The Pit).
* **The Law of Chromatic Penance:** Color is a scarce resource allocated exclusively to trauma. The world renders in desaturated monochrome by default. Saturation is unlocked only for `Blood` (Arterial Red), `Fire` (Searing Orange), and `Sickness` (Rotting Yellow). Blue is deleted from the sky.
* **The Law of Visual Friction (Particulate Z-Depth):** The air is never empty. Z-depth is choked with suspended particulate matter (ash, stone dust, oil soot). Optical clarity is forbidden because clarity implies safety.
* **The Law of No Bounce:** Global illumination is disabled. Mercy does not reflect. Light strikes a surface and dies there, casting hard-stencil shadows.
* **The Law of Silhouette:** Backlighting dominates key lighting; entities are defined as dark silhouettes cut against fire.

### B. Acoustic Laws & Sonic Semiotics
* **The Law of Percussive Dominance:** Audio events begin with instantaneous, square-wave transient spikes (Instant On $\rightarrow$ Instant Trauma $\rightarrow$ Metallic Decay). There are zero fade-ins.
* **The Law of the Drone:** Silence is illegal. When narrative action ceases, a low-frequency hum (60Hz electrical wire hum or tectonic sub-bass) rises to pressurize the eardrum.
* **The Law of Sonic Weight (Frequency Segregation):**
  * *High Frequencies (12kHz Tinnitus Shelf):* Punishment, nerve pain, and trauma.
  * *Low Frequencies (Sub-60Hz Rumble):* Divine authority, crushing mass, and judgment.
  * *Mids:* Scooped and suppressed (human dialogue is marginalized).
* **The Law of Trapped Decay:** Reverb models emulate `STONE_TOMB`—long, metallic, and enclosed, damping high frequencies instantly.

### C. Haptic & Tactile Commandments
* **The Law of Universal Abrasion:** No surface may be smooth. Friction coefficients are locked at maximum. Movement requires deliberate force; sliding is impossible. Touching any surface abrades skin.
  * *Smooth:* Deception / Trap.
  * *Rough:* Truth / Stone.
  * *Sharp:* Consequence / Metal.
  * *Wet:* Corruption / Blood.
* **The Law of Impact Recoil:** Every action triggers violent camera shake, haptic rumble, and visual blur. The user must experience the kinetic shockwave of their own exertion.
* **The Law of Thermal Hostility:** Temperature is aggressive. Heat creates severe mirage distortions; cold stiffens joint physics and reduces animation speeds.

### D. Atmospheric Chemistry (Olfaction)
* **The Law of Burnt Offerings:** Fresh air is a continuity violation. The atmosphere must permanently carry the olfactory signature of recent destruction: iron oxide (rust/blood, 40%), volcanic sulfur (30%), electrical ozone (20%), and sacrificial ash (10%).
* **The Law of Stagnation:** Liquids never flow freely; they pool, fester, and smell of rotting algae and dead marrow.

## 4. Material Physics & Constitution of Matter

* **The Law of Petrification (Wood / Organics):** Wood does not bend; it petrifies and snaps into shrapnel. Flesh does not bounce; it tears. Elasticity is zero across all substances.
* **The Law of Terminal Oxidation (Metal):** Metal exists in permanent decay. Iron is pitted; bronze is covered in verdigris. Spawning an object with a clean metallic sheen triggers a validation error.
* **The Law of Density (Earth / Stone):** Gravity acts with $1.5\times$ force on inorganic matter (`physics_weight: HEAVY`). Moving a stone requires heave animations; dropping an object creates an impact crater rather than a bounce.

## 6. Sustenance, Ontology & The Bestiary

### A. Dietary Laws
* **The Law of Bitterness:** Food sustains the body while actively denying sensory pleasure. Flavors are restricted to ash, salt, iron, and bitter herbs. Sweetness is treated as an auditory/visual hallucination.
* **The Law of Consumption:** Eating is an aggressive, wet, and exhausting physical struggle. Food items resist digestion.
* **The Law of Scarcity:** A piece of moldy bread is the baseline standard; discovering an unspoiled apple triggers a divine event.

### B. Ontology of Beings
* **The Law of Predation (Animals):** The domestic "Pet" flag is eradicated. Beasts are either desperate scavengers or ravenous predators viewing humans as meat.
* **The Law of the Watchers (Celestials):** Angels and spirits are incomprehensible wheels, wings, and burning eyes. Proximity automatically inflicts the `FEAR` status effect and destabilizes sensory rendering.
* **The Law of the Residue (Monsters):** Monsters are unclean errors of creation that cause render-engine glitches and leave persistent slime/rot trails.

## 7. Combat Kinetics, Wounds & Psychological Physics

### A. The Kinetics of Judgment
* **The Law of Consequential Damage:** Hit points are an illusion; wounds are the reality. Damage is an irrevocable state transition (`Hit` $\rightarrow$ `Bleeding`; `Crit` $\rightarrow$ `Crippled`). Visual damage meshes persist indefinitely.
* **The Law of Visceral Velocity (Hitstop):** Attacks carry crushing physical mass. Critical impacts enforce severe frame-freezing (hitstop) proportional to weapon mass, with camera snap-zooms into the point of rupture.
* **The Law of the Permanent Stain:** Blood and spent casings do not despawn. Battlefields remain scarred forever.

### B. Psychological Physics (The Emotional Spectrum)
* **The Law of Baseline Dread:** Fear is the default human state. In idle periods, the internal stress meter steadily rises; safety is a rapidly depleting resource.
* **The Law of Hysterical Strength:** Physical buffs unlock only under critical trauma and panic, accompanied by fisheye lens distortion and muted audio.
* **The Law of Hollow Victory:** Level-up fanfares are replaced by deep, ominous bronze gongs. End-of-mission screens display **"SURVIVED"** instead of **"VICTORY"**.
