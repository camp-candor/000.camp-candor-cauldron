## 1. Multi-Tiered Intelligence Tracking Framework

To stay ahead in generative fiction, spatial video consistency, and narrative engines, practitioners monitor both frontier academic research and applied room-level workflows:

  * `cs.CV` (Computer Vision): Video diffusion motion control, identity-preserving I2V, and trajectory conditioning.
  * `cs.AI` & `cs.HC` (Human-Computer Interaction): Mixed-initiative co-creative writing and multi-agent drama management.

## 2. Autonomous Agent Research: Gemini Deep Research Architecture

Unlike standard reactive search queries, agentic deep research conducts multi-step investigations over 5–15 minutes across dozens of sources:
* **Collaborative Planning:** Formulates and submits an editable multi-point research plan before scouring the web.
* **Iterative Deep Crawling:** Evaluates source credibility, follows technical citations, and adjusts queries dynamically.
* **Multi-Source Ingestion:** Cross-references open web sources with personal Google Drive, Gmail, and NotebookLM databases.

### Execution Blueprint: SOTA Character Continuity & Video Diffusion Brief
The exact operational brief configured to direct deep research agents to evaluate generative video continuity:
* **Target Areas:**
  1. *Underlying Architectures:* DiT mechanics, IP-Adapter, PhotoMaker, InstantID, video LoRAs, LivePortrait 3DMM guidance, and first/last frame latent interpolation.
  2. *Proprietary vs. Open Source:* Runway Gen-3/4, Sora, Veo, Kling, Luma vs. Wan 2.x, CogVideoX, HunyuanVideo, AnimateDiff.
  3. *Production Workflows:* Turnaround model sheets, ControlNet pose conditioning, ComfyUI face recovery passes (ReActor, CodeFormer), and NLE compositing.
  4. *Quantitative Metrics:* ArcFace cosine similarity, DINO tracking, FVD/FID, CLIP-I.
* **Deliverable Requirements:** Executive scorecard, algorithmic spatial/temporal deep-dive, practical ComfyUI node stack, failure taxonomy, and emerging research horizons.
