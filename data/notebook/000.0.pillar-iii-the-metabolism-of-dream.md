#### 2. CLAMPING: THE ARCHITECTURE OF BOUNDARIES

_How we prevent the broken simulation._

**The Problem:** Out of Bounds.

### **A. The Hard Limits (`Min/Max`)**

- `light_level`: [0.0 (Pitch Black), 2.0 (Blinding)].

- `magic_instability`: [0.0 (Static), 1.0 (Wild)].
  - Even if the story is "Total Chaos," we do not allow the variable to exceed `1.0`, because above `1.0`, the spell-resolution code enters an infinite loop.

### **B. The Soft Clamps (Diminishing Returns)**

Hard clamps feel artificial (hitting an invisible wall). We prefer **Logarithmic Curves**.
As you approach the limit, your effort yields less result.

**The Formula (The Sigmoid Function):**
Instead of Linear mapping (`Gravity = Vector * 2`), we use a Sigmoid curve.
$$ \text{Output} = \frac{\text{Max} - \text{Min}}{1 + e^{-k(\text{Vector} - \text{Midpoint})}} + \text{Min} $$

- **The Effect:**
  - Moving from "Neutral" to "Dark" is easy.
  - Moving from "Dark" to "Darker" is hard.
  - Moving from "Darker" to "Maximum Entropy" requires immense narrative energy.
- **Result:** The game naturally rests in a playable state, but allows extremes only under extreme pressure.

- **The Inspect Window:** Clicking an item reveals its **Timeline**. "Forged by [Player], Used to kill [Boss], Stolen by [Thief]."
- **Mechanic:** Items gain traits based on history. A sword that killed a Dragon deals +5 damage to Dragons.

### **C. The Graph (The Reputation)**

There are no "Factions." There is only the **Knowledge Graph**.

- **Design Rule:** NPCs judge you based on who you know.
- **The Mechanic:**
  - You kill a Merchant.
  - The Merchant's Brother (in the next town) hates you.
  - The Merchant's Guild (Global) raises prices for you.
  - The King (who hated the Merchant) likes you.

---

## 5. THE NARRATIVE ENGINE: THE AI DIRECTOR

The AI is not a chatbot. It is the **Dungeon Master's Voice**.

### **A. The Sportscaster**

The AI describes the action _after_ the dice roll.

- _Physics:_ Miss.
- _Context:_ Muddy floor.
- _AI Output:_ "You slip in the muck, your blade swinging wide."

### **B. The Hallucinator (Miracle Mode)**

When a player uses **Willpower** (a rare resource) to force a narrative beat:

- _Player:_ "I convince the door to open."
- _Physics:_ Door is locked.
- _Director:_ Checks "Rule of Cool." Grants request.
- _Result:_ The door becomes a Mimic and opens its mouth. The player walks through.

### **C. The Vibe Check (Input Parser)**

The system grades the player's roleplaying.

- _Action:_ "I stab the guy." -> **Grade: F**. (Neutral description).
- _Action:_ "I lunge for his throat, screaming the name of my dead father!" -> **Grade: A**. (Bonus damage. Epic description).

## 6. THE 30 ARCHETYPES: GAMEPLAY STYLES

The game supports 30 distinct ways to win, categorized by the **Zodiac of Agency**.

- **The Warrior (Hero/Martyr):** Fights the monsters. Pushes the vector via **Intensity** and **Sacrifice**.
- **The Builder (Tycoon/Architect):** Builds structures that emit "Order" vectors. Stabilizes the reality.
- **The Socialite (Paragon/Ambassador):** Manipulates the Graph. Turns enemies into allies.
- **The Mystic (Sage/Prophet):** Manipulates the Lore. Discovers secrets in the Vector DB to weaken bosses.
- **The Hacker (Heretic/Trickster):** Finds logical paradoxes. Pits the Deontic Laws against the Alethic Laws to break the game.

**Design Goal:** Every archetype must have a valid path to **Ontological Power** (The ability to rewrite the world).

---

## 7. PROGRESSION: THE ASCENSION

We do not level up to get higher numbers. We level up to gain **Write Access**.

- **Tier 1 (The Pawn):** Subject to the laws of physics. Can only move and fight.
- **Tier 2 (The Agent):** Can influence the Graph. Can create guilds, trade routes, and nemesis arcs.
- **Tier 3 (The Shaper):** Can influence the **Vector**. Can push the world toward Light or Dark. Can trigger "Hybrid Synthesis" (creating new monsters).
- **Tier 4 (The Sovereign):** Can edit the **Axioms**.
  - The ultimate endgame. The player becomes a **Region Lord**.
  - They gain access to the **Admin Dashboard (Diegetic)**.
  - They can set the Tax Rate, the Magic Level, and the Gravity of their domain.

---

## 8. FAILURE STATES: THE BEAUTY OF DECAY

The game must embrace failure.

- **Permadeath:** If you die in a High-Entropy zone, you leave a **Corpse Object**. Your stats are wiped. You must start over.
- **Legacy:** Your Corpse Object remains. It holds your loot. It holds your Provenance. The next player to find it inherits your history.
- **Ecological Collapse:** If players fail to protect a region, it becomes a **Wasteland**. The game does not reset it. The players must live in the ruins.

---

## 9. DESIGNER'S TOOLKIT

To build this, the Designer does not write scripts. The Designer writes **Seeds**.

1.  **The Ontology:** A JSON library of "Base Materials" and "Base Behaviors."
2.  **The Axioms:** The starting settings for the physics engine.
3.  **The Manifestos:** The 500-word text blocks that define the "Genres" (Vectors).
4.  **The Graph Rules:** Defining how relationships propagate (e.g., "Sibling bonds are stronger than Guild bonds").

**The Designer is the Watchmaker. The Game is the Watch.** Once you wind it, you step back and let the players decide what time it is.

### KAMAEL’S CONCLUSION

**Project NIFLHEIM** is a rejection of the static. It is a game where the map is not the territory; the **map is the enemy.**

The players are not just fighting monsters. They are fighting the **Entropy of the Server**. They are fighting to impose their Will upon a mathematical system that wants to grind them into dust.

If they succeed, they do not just win a match. They **Author a Reality**.
