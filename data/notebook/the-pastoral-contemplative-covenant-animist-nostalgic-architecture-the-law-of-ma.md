## 2. Linguistic Physics & The Lexicon of Breath

Narrative rhythm is meditative, prioritizing sensory micro-detail over grand abstractions:

### A. Core Linguistic Laws
1. **The Law of *Ma* (The Pregnant Space):**
   * *Mandate:* The empty space defines the matter; the pause defines the action.
   * *Enforcement:* Pacing mandates strict "Breathing Room" cycles. For every action or movement beat, there must be an alternating beat of eating, cleaning, resting, or observing the sky.
   * *Metric Cadence:* `largo_cantabile`—an unhurried, song-like flow.
2. **The Law of Oneiric Realism:**
   * *Mandate:* The world is more vivid and tactile than reality, yet softer.
   * *Enforcement:* Prose must focus on minute physical grounding: dust motes suspended in sunbeams, condensation trickling down cold glass, the specific patina of oxidized pipe rust.
3. **The Law of Gentle Gravity:**
   * *Mandate:* Objects carry density and mass, but the earth cradles them.
   * *Conversions:*
     * *Falling* $\longrightarrow$ Drifting / Tumbling.
     * *Running* $\longrightarrow$ Scrambling / Bounding.
     * *Wind* $\longrightarrow$ The Invisible Character.
     * *Magic* $\longrightarrow$ Natural Biological Function.

### A. The Photonic Engine
* **The Law of *Komorebi*:** Light is never harsh or raw; it is always diffracted through foliage, clouds, or wavy window panes.
* **The Gouache Spectrum:** Pure black (`#000000`) is banned. Shadow zones resolve as deep cobalt, twilight indigo, or dark violet.
* **The Law of Clutter (Maximum Cozy):** Clean, sterile rooms are soulless. Environments must be densely populated with micro-details: unwashed ceramic mugs, stacked notebooks, potted ferns, and worn kitchen spoons.

* **Water Refraction:** Water acts as liquid jewelry—crystal clear with high refraction, forming heavy, sparkling beads when splashed.

## 4. Acoustic Ecology & Atmospheric Chemistry

* **The Law of Tiny Sounds:** Intimate acoustic events are prioritized over explosions:
  * *Amplified:* Footsteps crushing grass, water pouring from ceramic pots, folding heavy paper, clicking brass gears.
  * *Suppressed:* Machine drone, synthetic hums, loud concussive blasts.
  * *Frequency Curve:* Warm mid-tones and soft, roll-off highs; sub-bass thump is culled.
* **The Law of Living Silence:** Silence is the sound of the world breathing: summer cicadas, gentle wind through tall grasses, rain ticking against a tin porch roof.
* **Sonic Semiotics:**
  * *The Creak:* Old wood or hemp rope straining under weight—signals reliability and age.
  * *The Gust:* A sudden wind swell—signals the arrival of change or a passing spirit.
* **Atmospheric Chemistry (Nostalgia):** Air composition is locked to memory: Petrichor (40%), Baking Yeast / Bread (30%), Coal/Steam Engine Oil (20%), and Crushed Green Leaves (10%).
