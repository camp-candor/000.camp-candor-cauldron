* **The Law of Saturation (The Solarpunk Spectrum):** Light is energy emanating from within surfaces. The dynamic range forbids crushed blacks. Shadows are not voids; they resolve as translucent hues of deep marine teal, radiant indigo, and soft violet.

* **Sonic Semiotics:**
