The Core Philosophy: Violence is neither stylized nor triumphant; it is Heavy, Messy, and Loud. It represents a catastrophic friction against the natural harmony (Ma). When a blade strikes, the air screams; when a machine fractures, it bleeds black oil like a slaughtered beast.The Law of Reluctance: The inception of conflict is hesitant; its conclusion is tragic. Combatants draw weapons slowly with trembling, white-knuckled grips and gritted teeth.The Law of the Curse: To kill is to invite metaphysical corruption. Malice does not remain abstract; it physically manifests as black smoke, writhing vermin, or violet miasma clinging to the striker's weapon and flesh.

7. Contextual Combat Modes & Narrative InterruptsA. Contextual Combat Styles:Human vs. Human (The Desperate Brawl): Clumsy, unglamorous survival. Combatants roll in the mud, lose weapons, pant, spit blood, and end with both sides emotionally hollowed out.Human vs. Machine (David and Goliath): The machine is an unyielding, slowly advancing mountain leaking steam and grinding internal gears; the human survives by scurrying up its frame, severing hydraulic lines, and prying loose armor plates.Human vs. Spirit (Negotiation of Force): Weapons pass through or deflect harmlessly from shifting ethereal bodies. The encounter resolves through spiritual pacification (the entity shifting color and relaxing) or tragic dissipation into the wind.B. Narrative Interrupt Mechanisms:The Weapon Break: When blades cross and lock, time decelerates. The camera punches in tight on the combatants' eyes, wind whips their hair, and dialogue triggers, humanizing the adversary.The Mask Reveal: If an enemy's helm or armor shatters, all score and ambient audio cut out instantly. The adversary is revealed as terrified, young, or agonizingly cursed, inducing hesitation in the attacker.C. Death Sequences & Transformative Dissolution:Machine Death: White steam billows $\longrightarrow$ internal muffled thuds rattle the hull $\longrightarrow$ the chassis collapses into the soil $\longrightarrow$ moss, wildflowers, and vines immediately sprout from the hot iron wreckage (the Laputa sequence).Cursed Death: Black corruption evaporates into greasy smoke $\longrightarrow$ the carcass disintegrates into dark mud or dry ash $\longrightarrow$ pristine, sun-bleached bones remain as permanent geographic markers.Spirit Death: The entity loses coherent form, breaking into floating geometric watercolor shapes $\longrightarrow$ releases a sudden gust of wind $\longrightarrow$ terminates in absolute, reverent silence.
