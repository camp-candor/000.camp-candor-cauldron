* **Stephen Marche (*Death of an Author*):**
  * *Technical Paradigm:* Paragraph-Level Stylistic Synthesis.

* **Gillian Sisley:**

  * *Contribution:* Segregates character dossiers into isolated context silos, physically walling off off-screen military/political events to prevent premature plot resolutions.
* **Tim Boucher (*The Lost Books Project*):**
  * *Technical Paradigm:* Database-Driven Epistolary Lore Weaving.
  * *Contribution:* Over 100 interconnected books in a shared universe. Uses LLMs as untrustworthy archivists drafting contradictory memos and redacted logs, turning model hallucinations into an in-universe puzzle box.
