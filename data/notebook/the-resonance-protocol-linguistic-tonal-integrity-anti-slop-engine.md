# The Resonance Protocol: Linguistic Tonal Integrity & Anti-Slop Engine

A specialized textual audit specification engineering language models (Claude 3.5 Sonnet, GPT-4o) to detect stylistic drift, eliminate "AI Slop" and clichéd prose patterns, enforce character idiolects, and verify era-specific lexicons.

---

## 1. System Philosophy: Countering "The Beige Plague"

The most insidious failure mode of long-horizon AI narrative is not logical contradiction, but **The Beige Plague**:
* **The "Helpful Assistant" Bias:** Unconstrained models default to an over-polite, hyper-symmetrical, and homogenizing register.
* **Tonal Collapse:** Gritty low-fantasy quietly slips into young-adult melodrama; an illiterate swamp-dweller suddenly speaks in balanced Victorian prose.
* **The Slop Tropes:** High-frequency generation clichés like *"a testament to"*, *"tapestry of"*, *"shiver down the spine"*, and *"delve into"* that betray synthetic origins.
* **The Resonance Role:** Operates as the **Grand Rhetorician**, evaluating not syntactic or grammatical correctness, but **Soul, Texture, and Voice**.

## 4. Nuance Analysis: The Logic of the Tongue

### 1. The Slop Filter & Probabilistic Suppression (Severity 1)
* **The Failure:** Models retrieve top-10 probability tokens that read like marketing copy (*tapestry*, *testament*, *unleash*, *delve*).
* **The Architectural Fix:** Banned token registers force the model into lower-probability, high-texture synonyms, immediately humanizing the prose.

### 2. Idiolect Enforcement (Severity 3)
* **The Failure:** In unconstrained AI writing, all characters sound like the model's base RLHF persona.
* **The Architectural Fix:** The `<character_profile>` establishes explicit voice anchors (e.g., *"Kael uses insect metaphors; omits subject pronouns; uses monosyllabic vocabulary"*). The auditor flags deviations and suggests in-voice alternatives (*"These people buzz like flies around a wound"* instead of *"These people are annoying"*).

### 3. Contextual Register & Interlocutor Awareness (Severity 2)
* **The Failure:** Characters maintaining a static diction regardless of social hierarchy or scene tension.
* **The Architectural Fix:** The `<narrative_context>` maps the relationship between speaker and `Interlocutor`. Slang delivered to a sovereign triggers a **Severity 2 Drift** or **Severity 3 Voice Break**, while the same slang delivered to a comrade passes cleanly.

### 4. Anachronism Detection (Severity 4 Heresy)
* Modern idioms (*"okay"*, *"cool"*, *"vibe"*, *"optimize"*) in historical or low-fantasy settings represent fatal immersion breaks. Categorized as Severity 4, triggering immediate build rejection.

### 5. The Pedagogical Rewrite Engine
* The JSON output requires a concrete `rewrite_suggestion` for every detected issue. The system does not merely reject prose; it provides the exact replacement string for the downstream compiler to apply via AST patching.
