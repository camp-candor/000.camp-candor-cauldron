## 3. Sensory Laws of the Sacred Veil

### A. Optical Laws & Chromatic Covenant
* **The Law of Binary Exposure:** Light is not illumination; it is exposure revealing sin. Highlights clip into blinding white heat (`#FFFFE0`), while unlit surfaces are culled to absolute black (`#000000`).
* **The Law of Chromatic Penance:** The world renders in desaturated monochrome by default. Saturation is a finite resource unlocked strictly for `Blood` (Arterial Red), `Fire` (Searing Orange), and `Sickness` (Rotting Yellow). Blue is deleted from the sky.
* **The Law of Visual Friction (Particulate Z-Depth):** The air is a medium of physical resistance. Z-depth is choked with suspended dust, smoke, and ash. Optical clarity is forbidden because clarity implies safety.
* **The Law of No Bounce:** Global illumination is disabled. Mercy does not reflect. Light strikes surfaces and dies there, casting hard-stencil shadows.
* **The Law of Silhouette:** Backlighting dominates key lighting; entities are defined as dark silhouettes cut against fire.

### B. Auditory, Tactile & Olfactory Laws
* **Auditory Trauma & Percussive Dominance:** Sound arrives as a concussive shockwave with instantaneous square-wave attack envelopes.
  * *The Drone:* Silence is illegal. An underlying 60Hz hum or tectonic rumble pressurizes the eardrum during idle states.
  * *Sonic Weight:* High frequencies (12kHz tinnitus ring) signify punishment and nerve trauma; low frequencies (sub-bass) signify divine authority. Mid frequencies (the human voice) are scooped.
  * *Decay:* Reverb models `STONE_TOMB`—long, metallic, and enclosed, damping high frequencies immediately.
* **Tactile Abrasion & Haptic Commandments:** No surface may be smooth. Friction coefficients are locked at maximum. To touch a surface is to be abraded.
  * *Smooth:* Deception / Trap.
  * *Rough:* Truth / Stone.
  * *Sharp:* Consequence / Metal.
  * *Wet:* Corruption / Blood.
  * *Impact Recoil:* Every action shakes the camera, rumbles the haptics, and blurs vision. Temperature is aggressive (heat mirages vs. cold joint stiffness).
* **Olfactory Residue:** The air permanently smells of recent destruction: iron oxide (rust/blood, 40%), volcanic sulfur (30%), electrical ozone (20%), and sacrificial ash (10%). Liquids pool, fester, and smell of stagnant rot.

## 4. Material Physics & Constitution of Matter

* **The Law of Petrification (Wood / Organics):** Wood does not bend; it petrifies and snaps into shrapnel. Flesh does not bounce; it tears. Elasticity is zero across all substances.
* **The Law of Terminal Oxidation (Metal):** Metal exists in permanent decay. Iron is pitted; bronze is covered in verdigris. Spawning an object with a clean metallic sheen triggers a validation error.
* **The Law of Density (Earth / Stone):** Gravity acts with $1.5\times$ force on inorganic matter (`physics_weight: HEAVY`). Moving a stone requires heave animations; dropping an object creates an impact crater rather than a bounce.

## 6. Narrative Physics, Combat Kinetics & Emotional Spectrum

### A. The Four Laws of Narrative Physics
1. *Law of Momentum:* Narratives crash rather than conclude; conversations interrupt; movement fights inertia.
2. *Law of Decay:* Tools jam, weapons fracture, bodies tire, and systems degrade continuously.
3. *Law of Consequence:* Actions leave permanent scars; kicked-down doors remain broken forever.
4. *Law of Revelation:* Truth is terrifying; peeling back mysteries reveals threatening, cosmic scale.

### B. Combat Kinetics of Judgment
* *Law of Consequential Damage:* Hit points are an illusion; damage is an irrevocable state transition (`Hit` $\rightarrow$ `Bleeding`; `Crit` $\rightarrow$ `Crippled`). Visual damage meshes persist indefinitely.
* *Law of Visceral Velocity (Hitstop):* Attacks carry crushing mass. Critical impacts enforce severe frame-freezing (hitstop) proportional to weapon mass, with camera snap-zooms into the point of rupture.
* *Law of the Permanent Stain:* Blood and spent casings do not despawn. Battlefields remain scarred forever.
* *Pain Response:* The body is a machine that breaks; damage causes stumbling, seizing, and loss of motor control rather than simple flinching.
