| Beat | Intensity | Grammar Note |

| 3 | `7/10` | **PEAK** — Ideological argument; zero physical action |
| 4 | `3/10` | Tacit agreement, gift of woven cord; verbal parting |

### Diagnostic Takeaway
By adding the `Silent?` and `Dialog?` tracking rows, the curve proves that 505 is the exact **structural photonegative** of the rest of the series:
* **Zero silent holds** across the entire 4-act structure.
* The peak (`7/10`) rests entirely on verbal confrontation rather than kinetic animation.
* Surfaced directly in the data as an intentional anomaly, preventing downstream teams from misinterpreting the script as a tone error.
